Modelence is a production cloud for your backend, databases and scheduled jobs. It deploys your app
and keeps operating it after that: crashed processes are restarted, traffic is rerouted when an
instance fails and capacity is added under load, and you get a report of what happened.

Apps run on AWS in the region you choose. Every environment gets a MongoDB Atlas database, and your
app can declare managed PostgreSQL databases and Redis caches next to it. Logs, metrics and job runs
are collected from the first deploy, and production databases are [backed up](/cloud/backups)
automatically.

## Get started

The quickest way to deploy is to hand it to your coding agent. Paste this into Claude Code, Cursor or
Codex in your project:

```text
Deploy using https://modelence.com/skill.md
```

The agent reads your project, writes `modelence.config.json` to describe what it needs, and deploys
it. You can also sign up at [cloud.modelence.com](https://cloud.modelence.com) and connect your
repository from the dashboard.
